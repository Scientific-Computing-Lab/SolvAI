\subsection*{Does the response help beyond one endpoint?}

The matched test raises a second question: does the information in the 15 frozen
descriptors remain useful when the hydration learner changes? We tested nine
fixed endpoint configurations, from trees and neural networks to molecular
graphs and pretrained encodings (Table~\ref{tab:endpoints}). For each one, a
paired fit removes only the response columns while retaining its selected
structural inputs and training recipe. Thus the comparison asks both how accurate
the complete learners are and what the responses contribute within each learner.
TabM uses a shared-parameter neural ensemble, and TabPFN predicts from a
pretrained transformer conditioned on labelled training rows.\cite{tabm2025,tabpfn2025,tabpfn35}
The study is retrospective; configurations were selected using training-side
validation, with the full protocol in Methods and Supplementary Methods~S18.

The lowest full-model point estimate changes with the cohort: TabPFN reaches
0.199 kcal mol$^{-1}$ on ARROW-85, a MoLFormer neural head 1.111 on External-220,
and ridge plus a neural residual 1.429 on Strict-97 (Table~\ref{tab:endpoints}).
These are descriptive rankings of complete predictors. They cannot tell us
whether the same predictor needed the response descriptors.

The paired fits answer that question (Fig.~\ref{fig:endpoints}). On External-220
and its nested Strict-97 subset, seven of nine configurations have below-zero
descriptive intervals for the response-minus-no-response MAE difference. The
neural MoLFormer endpoint, for example, changes from 1.332 to 1.111 externally;
ExtraTrees, message passing and several other learners also benefit. On ARROW-85,
the intervals are below zero for ExtraTrees and the two MoLFormer endpoints.
The contrast has exceptions that make the distinction useful: TabPFN has the
lowest ARROW point estimate without a resolved response increment, while the
lowest full-model Strict-97 configuration, ridge plus a neural residual, has
lower error when its responses are removed. The representation can therefore
help across architectures without defining a single best complete model.
Complete paired results, mechanism diagrams and selection variants are in
Supplementary Tables~13--15, Figs.~8--10 and Data~6.
