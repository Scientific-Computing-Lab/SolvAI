Physical response becomes reusable molecular supervision. External calculations and measurements describe complementary aspects of transfer into water. Structure-to-response surrogates distil these sources into 15 compact, molecule-aligned coordinates. A separate experimentally supervised endpoint combines the predicted responses with molecular fingerprints and descriptors. At deployment, a SMILES string produces a hydration free energy without MD, PIMD or a probe calculation. On the matched 85-solute ARROW chemistry, response priors reduce MAE from 0.303 to 0.202 kcal mol^-1; shuffling the priors abolishes the gain. ARROW/PIMD8 (0.205 kcal mol^-1) is shown only as an accuracy reference and is not a retained teacher.
