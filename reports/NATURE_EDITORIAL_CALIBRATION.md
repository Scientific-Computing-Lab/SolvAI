# Nature Portfolio editorial and visual calibration

Checked 27 August 2026 against current Nature guidance and three recent
research articles selected for their combination of molecular science,
simulation and machine learning:

- Abramson *et al.*, “Accurate structure prediction of biomolecular
  interactions with AlphaFold 3”, *Nature* (2024),
  [10.1038/s41586-024-07487-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07487-w).
- Merchant *et al.*, “Scaling deep learning for materials discovery”,
  *Nature* (2023),
  [10.1038/s41586-023-06735-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06735-9).
- Zhang *et al.*, “Predicting equilibrium distributions for molecular systems
  with deep learning”, *Nature Machine Intelligence* (2024),
  [10.1038/s42256-024-00837-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-024-00837-3).

This is editorial calibration, not an assertion that the studies use the same
method or evidence standard as SolvAI.

## Visual grammar extracted

- Main figures answer scientific questions. Concept figures are integrated
  with data or molecular examples; workflow bookkeeping is not given a whole
  main figure.
- Four or five main display items are typical. Related evidence is assembled
  in compact multi-panel figures with aligned panel edges and little internal
  prose.
- Molecular objects and measured relationships carry the narrative. Colour is
  semantic and stable across panels; annotations are short.
- Aggregate metrics are accompanied by item-level evidence, uncertainty or
  representative molecular examples.
- Robustness and limitations appear after the central result. They define
  scope without displacing the discovery from the opening visual sequence.
- Supplementary Figures preserve methodological and diagnostic depth without
  duplicating main panels.

## Nature Communications implementation

- The revised submission uses a concise explicit abstract appropriate to Nature
  Communications.
- Main-text subheadings remain below 40 characters.
- The main article uses four display figures and superscript numeric citations.
- Five Supplementary Figures are embedded in the single Supplementary Information
  PDF, each followed immediately by its legend, and every item is cited in the article.
- The discontinued selective-PIMD diagnostic and redundant comparison table are not
  part of the submission package.
- Large tabular records are supplied as machine-readable Supplementary Data,
  rather than compressed into an illegible manuscript table.
