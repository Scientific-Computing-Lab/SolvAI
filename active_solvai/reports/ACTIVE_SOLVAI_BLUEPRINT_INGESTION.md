## Scientific thesis

The frozen structure-only SolvAI model supplies a molecule-conditioned prior over hydration and solvation response. Active SolvAI tests whether a small, prospectively chosen set of **actual response observations from the query molecule** reveals the molecule-specific residual that structure alone cannot infer. If it does, those observations may update the response curve and experimental endpoint more efficiently than a fixed dense simulation protocol. The thesis is not that adaptivity must win: a fixed sparse schedule is allowed to be the scientifically correct result.

## What the previous SolvAI paper established

The evidence-corrected SolvAI release established that 15 molecule-aligned, structure-predicted response coordinates improve a matched experimentally supervised hydration endpoint on ARROW-85 (0.30335 to 0.20223 kcal mol-1), that the gain disappears under response shuffling, survives repeated partitions and chemical-separation controls, and transfers in direction on endpoint-disjoint Tier-A chemistry. Its deployment consumes only a SMILES and does not run MD, PIMD or a probe calculation. PIMD8 is a comparator, not a retained teacher.

## What it did not establish

It did not show that its ensemble spread is a calibrated uncertainty, that it has a per-query applicability score, that structure-predicted PIMD2 response improves the endpoint, that sparse target-molecule measurements can reconstruct hidden alchemical windows, that adaptive allocation beats a fixed schedule, that stopping remains calibrated under optional sampling, or that any Active SolvAI policy transfers to a blind external cohort.

## Why the negative predicted-PIMD2 result is not decisive here

The earlier experiment passed **predicted** PIMD2 quantities from a structure model into the endpoint. It therefore measured whether structure could reconstruct those high-fidelity response targets accurately enough for downstream use. Active SolvAI instead supplies the held-out molecule’s **actual** observation and tests its surprise relative to the frozen prior. The measured residual can contain molecule-specific information that is absent from every structure-only representation, so the previous negative does not answer this question.

## Two distinct targets

1. **Same-Hamiltonian simulation reconstruction.** Predict hidden dH/dlambda windows and the dense integral generated by the same Hamiltonian/protocol. Metrics include hidden-window error, integral error, maximum local error and interval calibration.
2. **Experimental hydration free energy.** Improve held-out experimental Delta G_hyd beyond frozen no-simulation SolvAI. This target includes Hamiltonian bias and experimental/model discrepancy, so success or failure cannot be inferred from reconstruction alone.

The two target families are never collapsed into a single metric or claim.

## Three complete research directions

- **A — empirical probe-residual correction:** use actual-minus-prior response residuals to correct the experimental endpoint under nested molecule-held-out evaluation.
- **B — molecule-conditioned Bayesian quadrature:** use SolvAI/structure to define a curve prior, condition on actual observations, reconstruct hidden windows and integrate with calibrated uncertainty.
- **C — adaptive multi-fidelity allocation:** choose among zero simulation, sparse classical probes, PIMD2, trajectory extension/new lambda points and limited higher-bead sentinels by expected uncertainty reduction per measured cost.

All three directions are bounded. No learned potential, new Hamiltonian, acquisition rule and endpoint model may be changed simultaneously in the first decisive experiment.

## Matched comparators and destructive controls

Phase 1 compares the same eligible molecules and splits for frozen SolvAI, historical/reproduced predicted PIMD2, raw actual PIMD2, actual-minus-predicted residual, every one/two/three-point subset of {0.1, 0.5, 0.9}, shuffled actual observations, posterior conditioning, direct integration and simple fixed/generic alternatives. Any preprocessing, response model, calibration or hyperparameter decision is confined to outer training molecules.

Replay must compare frozen fixed, uniform, random, curvature-only, generic Bayesian quadrature, SolvAI-conditioned Bayesian quadrature, adaptive multi-fidelity and a clearly non-deployable oracle against the dense reference at identical observed cost. A simpler fixed schedule may win.

## Exposure and leakage rules

Molecular identity uses submitted and canonical isomeric SMILES, full InChIKey, connectivity block, fragment parent, uncharged parent and canonical tautomer, plus registry identifiers when present. The held-out molecule and its chemical group are excluded from response fitting, endpoint calibration, kernel/acquisition selection and stopping-threshold selection as required by the outer regime. Experimental labels are read only for final endpoint scoring. Lambda locations, trajectory fractions, molecules, exclusions and claims may not be selected using held-out error.

Tier-A is already viewed and is development evidence only. Tier-B may not be constructed, opened or scored until method, policy, stopping, identity, target, analysis and interpretation are frozen and the principal investigator explicitly approves the blind campaign. Endpoint-disjoint and strict Active-disjoint Tier-B subsets must be reported separately.

## Compute policy

Existing evidence is exhausted before new production simulation. CPU is used for identity, RDKit, trees/small GPs, bootstraps, I/O, figures and LaTeX. GPU is reserved for workloads that benefit from it. Every new protocol begins with restartable smoke tests and records force evaluations, simulated time, bead-windows, GPU/CPU hours, wall time and failed work. The local exploratory envelope is approximately one day of one RTX 3090; paid cloud, new credentials, multi-GPU scale, a broad production campaign or Tier-B requires approval.

## Decision criteria

- **Phase 0 exit:** parent metrics/smoke predictions reproduce; every available response has stable identity and raw/derived status; possible retrospective replays are explicit.
- **Phase 1 go:** median endpoint improvement at least 0.02 kcal mol-1 across repeated partitions with a paired interval below zero, **or** substantial calibrated dense-reconstruction gain even if endpoint labels limit improvement.
- **Phase 1 kill:** endpoint gain below 0.01 kcal mol-1 together with no meaningful dense-reconstruction gain and unstable signs across chemistry. A favorable family, split or lambda point cannot rescue a failed gate.
- **Replay go:** molecule-conditioned/adaptive method credibly matches or beats strongest fixed, random and generic comparators at measured cost, with calibration evaluated at actual stopping time.
- **Pilot return:** at least one complete method survives Phase 1, replay, destructive controls, chemical/repeated validation, calibration and prospective sentinels; remaining uncertainty is principally statistical scale or chemical coverage.
- **No-go return:** all predeclared routes meet kill criteria, or a hard external dependency makes the decisive experiment impossible. The report must separate signal absence, noise, placement, trajectory length, cross-fidelity failure, Hamiltonian bias, endpoint limitations, data absence and implementation failure.
- **Scale request:** state the frozen protocol, molecule-level paired effects and intervals, reconstruction, endpoint, calibration, force evaluations, ps, bead-windows, GPU-hours, wall time, failed-work cost, pilot result, projected scale and exact claim enabled.

## Anticipated permitted deviations

1. **Hardware count:** the actual host exposes 6 physical/12 logical CPU cores, 62 GiB RAM, one RTX 3090 (24 GiB) and 116 GiB currently free, rather than the earlier approximate 22-core description. CPU parallelism and disk safeguards will use measured resources.
2. **Historical sparse protocol:** the existing PIMD2 data are three nominal lambda points with 5 ps production and two beads. They support the actual-observation endpoint gate and time-block analysis, but not dense hidden-window reconstruction. Direction B/Phase 2 will proceed only if compatible dense historical curves are found or generated after the gate.
3. **Bayesian implementation:** `gpytorch` and OpenMM are not installed in the parent release environment. Initial low-dimensional GP/Bayesian-quadrature work can use SciPy/scikit-learn; any added dependency will be locked in the Active environment and justified before use.
4. **Private data:** raw probe logs and trajectories are authorized locally but are not assumed redistributable. The public workstream will initially store hashes, manifests, derived summaries where permitted and reconstruction commands rather than raw private trajectories.

No scientific threshold, comparison, split or exposure rule is weakened by these deviations. Further deviations require a dated amendment stating what results were already visible.
