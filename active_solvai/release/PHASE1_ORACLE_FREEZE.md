## Scientific question

Do actual PIMD2 response observations from a held-out target molecule, or their residual relative to a structure-predicted response, contain molecule-specific information that improves the evidence-corrected frozen SolvAI endpoint?

## Primary hypothesis and estimand

The primary candidate is a fold-local ridge correction to frozen SolvAI using the **actual-minus-structure-predicted total SYSTEM dH/dlambda residual at all three nominal lambda points**. The primary estimand is

`mean_i(|y_i - active_i| - |y_i - frozen_solvai_i|)`

in kcal mol-1 on the same 72 complete molecules. Negative values favour Active SolvAI. Each molecule’s absolute error is averaged across the five repeated partitions before the 100,000-resample paired bootstrap.

## Data and exclusions

- Cohort: the 72 neutral ARROW water-solvation molecules with successful PIMD2 observations at all three nominal lambda values.
- Observations: 5 ps production, two beads, nominal lambda 0.1/0.5/0.9, native Arbalest annihilation dH/dlambda in kcal mol-1.
- Primary response: total `dHdL` in the `SYSTEM.ene` output.
- Secondary component vector: `dHdL_Coul`, `dHdL_Coul_SC`, `dHdL_VdW`, `dHdL_VdW_SC`, `dHdL_LRCor`, and `dHdL_PME`. Identically zero valence/polarization columns are inventoried but excluded prospectively from the component model.
- Eligibility/exclusion: fixed by `DATA_AND_IDENTITY_FREEZE.md`; no error-based exclusion.
- Partial curves: the four incomplete probe molecules are excluded from every primary comparison and may appear only in a labelled missingness diagnostic.

## Splits and nesting

The primary fixed split is inherited from `fold_random`. Five full repeated five-fold partitions use seeds 314159, 271828, 161803, 141421 and 173205. The exact assignments are frozen in `data/manifests/probe_split_assignments.csv`.

For each outer fold:

1. The held-out molecule’s experimental label and actual response are absent from all training and selection.
2. Frozen SolvAI outer-test predictions are generated by the exact corrected endpoint configuration using public labels plus outer-training ARROW labels only.
3. Correction targets on outer-training molecules use inner-OOF SolvAI predictions generated without the outer-test fold and without the inner-held-out molecule.
4. Structure-to-PIMD2 predictions for outer-test molecules are fitted on outer-training response curves only. Outer-training response predictions are themselves inner-OOF.
5. Correction regularization is selected only from outer-training inner folds.

The fixed and five repeat partitions are primary. Family/scaffold assignments are used as correction-level group-holdout diagnostics if every held-out group leaves at least 30 complete response curves for fitting. These diagnostics do not replace the parent paper’s globally separated endpoint evaluation.

## Frozen models

### Frozen SolvAI baseline

- Inputs: 2,265 deterministic structure features plus the 15 corrected/frozen response priors.
- Endpoint: three `ExtraTreesRegressor` members, 360 trees each, seeds 11/29/47, squared-error criterion, `max_features=0.7`, `min_samples_leaf=2`, `min_samples_split=2`, unlimited depth, no bootstrap.
- Public endpoint rows: 1,280, weight 1.
- available outer-training ARROW rows: weight 3.
- Median imputation with training-only missingness indicators.

The implementation must reproduce the stored outer-test SolvAI predictions within floating-point tolerance before scoring a correction.

### Structure-to-PIMD2 response prior

- Input: the same 2,265 deterministic structure features.
- Target: the three total SYSTEM dH/dlambda values; the component analysis uses the predeclared six-component vector at the same points.
- Model: median imputation followed by a multi-output `ExtraTreesRegressor` ensemble with 360 trees/member, seeds 11/29/47, `max_features=0.7`, `min_samples_leaf=3`, `min_samples_split=2`, unlimited depth and no bootstrap.
- No experimental endpoint labels enter response fitting.

### Endpoint correction

- Target: experimental endpoint residual `y_exp - y_frozen_solvai_inner_oof` on outer-training molecules.
- Model: `StandardScaler` plus ridge regression with intercept.
- Alpha candidates: 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100 and 1000.
- Selection: lowest concatenated inner-fold MAE of `frozen prediction + correction`; exact ties choose the larger alpha.
- Final correction: refit at selected alpha on all outer-training rows and applied to outer test.
- No clipping, family-specific model, test-driven filtering or coefficient-sign constraint.

## Shuffled control

The actual response row is permuted jointly across molecules, separately within outer training and outer test, preserving the three-point/component covariance and feature count. Seeds are 88001–88005. The endpoint target is never used to define the permutation. Results are reported individually and as their mean prediction.

## Posterior conditioning

Two Gaussian three-point curve models are fixed:

1. **Generic:** outer-training mean and Ledoit–Wolf covariance of raw total response.
2. **Structure-conditioned:** outer-training mean and Ledoit–Wolf covariance of actual-minus-inner-OOF structure predictions, added to the outer-test structure-predicted mean.

Observed-point noise is the squared five-contiguous-block SEM from the corresponding held-out trajectory prefix. A numerical jitter of `1e-8 * max(trace(K)/3, 1)` is added only for matrix stability. The posterior conditions on a predeclared lambda subset and predicts the other points. Normal marginal intervals at 50%, 80%, 90% and 95% are evaluated only for unobserved points. This is sparse held-point reconstruction, not dense-curve validation.

## Numerical integration

The three-point annihilation proxy is

`I = 0.1*y(0.1) + (0.8/6)*(y(0.1) + 4*y(0.5) + y(0.9)) + 0.1*y(0.9)`.

Hydration uses `-I`. For incomplete subsets, P1-F integrates the posterior mean. Any affine mapping to experimental hydration is fitted only on outer-training molecules. PIMD8 comparison is descriptive because the observations are PIMD2 and only three short windows.

## Metrics and uncertainty

- Endpoint primary: MAE.
- Endpoint secondary: RMSE, median absolute error, fraction improved and molecule-level paired error change.
- Reconstruction: hidden-point MAE/RMSE, posterior interval width and empirical coverage at 50/80/90/95%.
- Integration: MAE against experimental Delta G_hyd and against PIMD8, reported separately.
- Bootstrap: 100,000 molecule-level paired resamples, seed 20260828. Repeated-partition inference averages absolute error per molecule before resampling.
- Stability: MAE and response shift at sequential trajectory prefixes 10%, 20%, 40%, 70% and 100%; no noncontiguous future block may be selected.

## Interpretation rules

- **Positive endpoint gate:** the primary P1-D median MAE improvement is at least 0.020 kcal mol-1 across the five repeat partitions, the repeated molecule-paired 95% interval is wholly below zero, and P1-D is better than the mean shuffled-residual control with an interval below zero.
- **Neutral:** directionally consistent improvement of 0.010–0.020, or improvement at least 0.020 with an interval crossing zero, or dependence on one partition/chemical group.
- **Negative endpoint gate:** primary improvement below 0.010, worsening, or unstable sign across repeats.
- **Reconstruction alternative go:** only a substantial hidden-point improvement over generic conditioning with credible interval calibration. Because no dense population exists, this cannot by itself authorize a dense-replay claim; it can justify a prospective dense sentinel request.
- **Kill:** endpoint improvement below 0.010, no useful held-point reconstruction gain, and unstable chemistry/repeat effects. No favorable lambda subset, component vector or family may rescue the primary claim.

## Conclusions the analysis will not support

This gate cannot establish dense PIMD8 reconstruction, an unbiased free-energy estimator, prospective optional-stopping calibration, broad chemical generalization, a fixed speedup, superiority to PIMD8, or Tier-B transfer. It cannot turn the PIMD2 protocol-dependent intermediate curve into an intrinsic molecular observable.
