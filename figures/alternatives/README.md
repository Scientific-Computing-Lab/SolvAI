# Figure 1 composition study

Three native Penpot compositions were evaluated at 180-mm width before freezing
Figure 1:

- `fig1_variant_A_minimal`: maximum whitespace, but insufficient emphasis on the
  molecular character of the response supervision.
- `fig1_variant_B_molecular`: real Packmol solute--water coordinates and stronger
  chemistry, but excessive emphasis on one solvent configuration and on the
  non-retained lambda experiment.
- `fig1_variant_C_balanced`: selected design. Its files live in `figures/main/`
  and are copied to `fig1_concept.*` for manuscript compatibility.

The current alternatives are installed from `figures/penpot/exports/`. Scientific
provenance, the editable `.penpot` source and the selection rationale are recorded
in [`../penpot/FIG1_BUILD_NOTES.md`](../penpot/FIG1_BUILD_NOTES.md).
