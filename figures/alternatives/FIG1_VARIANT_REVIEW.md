# Figure 1 art-direction review

All three candidates use the same frozen scientific content, 15-prior definition,
model boundary and 180-mm by 100-mm canvas. They differ only in visual hierarchy.

| Composition | Principal strength | Reason not selected |
|---|---|---|
| A — Minimal Nature-style | Fastest overview and most whitespace | Compresses the chemistry and makes the response sources feel generic |
| B — Molecular-science-forward | Strongest molecular presence; uses the real Packmol solvent configuration | Gives disproportionate visual weight to an illustrative solvent snapshot and to the non-retained PIMD2 lambda diagnostic |
| C — Balanced | Preserves four grounded response-source vignettes, presents the exact priors as a connected taxonomy, and gives deployment a simple uninterrupted path | Selected |

The selected design meets the central communication test: physical and
physicochemical response information is generated once on the left, transformed
into 15 named structure-predicted coordinates in the centre, and reused from SMILES
alone on the right. ARROW/PIMD8 remains a visually isolated comparator.

The `*_print_180mm.png` files are the publication-size review renders. Full
scientific provenance is in
[`../source/FIG1_BUILD_NOTES.md`](../source/FIG1_BUILD_NOTES.md).
