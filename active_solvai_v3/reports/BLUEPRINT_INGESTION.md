## Scientific thesis

The fixed 15-point alchemical grid is retained. Every window receives the same
mandatory initial sampling floor. Active SolvAI v3 asks whether frozen molecular
response coordinates improve the **allocation of additional immutable trajectory
chunks** beyond a competent adaptive allocator that sees only the target simulation's
observed diagnostics. The primary estimand is same-Hamiltonian integral error or cost,
scored on an independent trajectory stream. Experimental hydration labels are not
inputs, targets or development feedback for the primary analysis.

## Inherited evidence and boundary

SolvAI established that predicted response coordinates improve an experimentally
supervised structure-only hydration endpoint. Active SolvAI v1 found no endpoint value
from three actual 5-ps PIMD2 observations and its prospective molecule-conditioned
lambda-placement policy lost to uniform direct integration. The v2 independent-noise
audit showed that the attractive same-curve oracle was selection-biased, and a
prospective independent-replica campaign was stopped before simulation because its
power and reference-reliability gates failed. V3 does not reinterpret those results.
It asks a different question: whether heterogeneous **sampling effort** on a fixed
grid is predictable and allocable.

## Required causal contrasts

1. Generic observed-data-only adaptive allocation versus equal-time allocation.
2. SolvAI-conditioned adaptive allocation versus the matched generic allocator.
3. Aligned versus molecule-shuffled SolvAI coordinates and response-removal controls.

Generic adaptation alone is not an Active SolvAI result.

## Information firewall

- Policies may use only frozen molecular inputs, already revealed target-trajectory
  prefixes, λ/protocol metadata and pre-frozen training information.
- Allocation on stream A is scored against hidden stream B, then directions reverse.
- Contiguous blocks of one trajectory are complementary development diagnostics, not
  independent replicas or prospective validation.
- Molecules and standardized equivalents stay in one fold.
- Experimental endpoint labels cannot select molecules, policies, hyperparameters,
  budgets, stopping rules or exclusions.
- All earlier ARROW, Tier-A and dense-sentinel molecules are development-exposed.

## Gates

The zero-new-simulation gate must first show that early diagnostics predict later
complementary/independent difficulty and that SolvAI coordinates add held-out value
beyond λ/protocol, generic observed diagnostics and ordinary structure. If existing
data cannot answer this, a new development campaign is allowed only after a committed
power/reliability freeze. A final policy and genuinely new sentinel receive separate
committed freezes. Scale is allowed only after a prospectively frozen independent
sentinel resolves the SolvAI-over-generic contrast or after a quantitative gate
identifies the minimum answerable design and cost.

## Anticipated deviations

No deviation is currently authorized. The inherited dense library has one 5-ps
trajectory per molecule-window, so its complementary blocks can support development
identifiability and power planning but cannot satisfy the final independent-stream
criterion. Any use of these blocks will be labelled accordingly.
