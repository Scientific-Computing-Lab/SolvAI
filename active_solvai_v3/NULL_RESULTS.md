## V3-NR-001 — response coordinates do not identify sampling difficulty

Under the preregistered 12-molecule Gate-1 analysis, the SolvAI-conditioned
model had a complementary log-difficulty MAE of 1.475 versus 1.427 for the
matched generic observed-only model. The paired difference was +0.0477 (90% CI
-0.0171 to +0.1199), and the aligned prior was indistinguishable from five
molecule-shuffled controls. The direction was not favorable at any fixed prefix
(0.5, 1, 2 or 3 ps). This result blocks a v3 simulation launch unless a
prospective power/reliability calculation supports a scientifically and
computationally credible independent design.
